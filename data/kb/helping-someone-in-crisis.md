---
title: Helping Someone in Crisis
source: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — Help Someone Else
url: https://988lifeline.org/help-someone-else/
---
If you're worried about someone, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline says to pay attention to changes: behavior that is new, has increased, or seems linked to a painful event, loss or change can be a sign they may be at risk.

Five action steps from 988:

1. **Ask.** Ask directly and calmly whether they are thinking about suicide.
2. **Be there.** Listen and accept their feelings without judging or debating whether they are right or wrong.
3. **Help keep them safe.** Reduce access to things they could use to hurt themselves.
4. **Help them connect.** Encourage them to reach out to 988 or another source of support.
5. **Follow up.** Check in after the crisis to show you still care.

Try not to act shocked, dare them, or offer empty reassurance — instead, gently offer hope that other options exist. Never agree to keep a plan to hurt themselves a secret. Listen actively: let them finish, ask questions, and reflect back what you heard. Supporting someone can be hard, so reach out for support for yourself too.

In the U.S., you can call or text **988**, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org, any time — for yourself or for someone you're worried about. If someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services right away.
