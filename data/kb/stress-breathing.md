Slow, deep breathing can help calm the body's stress response. A simple practice:

1. Get comfortable — sitting, lying down, or standing with feet hip-width apart.
2. Let your breath flow as deep down into your belly as is comfortable, without forcing it.
3. Breathe in gently through your nose, counting steadily from 1 to 5.
4. Without pausing or holding your breath, let it flow out gently, counting from 1 to 5 again.
5. Keep doing this for 3 to 5 minutes.

Practising regularly, for example every day, tends to bring the most benefit. Breathing exercises are a self-help technique and not a substitute for professional support if stress feels unmanageable.
