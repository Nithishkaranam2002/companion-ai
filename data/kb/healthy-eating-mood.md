Eating a balanced, varied diet helps you get the nutrients you need and supports overall wellbeing. The NHS offers eight practical tips:

1. **Base meals on higher-fibre starchy foods** such as wholegrain bread, wholewheat pasta, brown rice or potatoes with skins. They help you feel fuller for longer.
2. **Eat lots of fruit and vegetables** — aim for at least 5 portions a day. Fresh, frozen, canned and dried all count.
3. **Eat more fish**, aiming for two portions a week including one oily fish such as salmon, mackerel or sardines.
4. **Cut down on saturated fat and sugar.** Choose lean meat and unsaturated fats like vegetable oils, and use food labels to spot foods high in sugar.
5. **Eat less salt.** Much of it is already in processed foods like bread, cereals, soups and sauces.
6. **Get active and aim for a healthy weight**, which lowers the risk of many serious conditions.
7. **Stay hydrated.** Water, lower-fat milk and lower-sugar drinks, including tea and coffee, are healthier choices than sugary soft drinks.
8. **Don't skip breakfast.** A breakfast higher in fibre and lower in fat, sugar and salt, such as wholegrain cereal with fruit, can be part of a balanced diet.

Regular meals also help keep your energy levels steadier through the day.

If you're worried about your weight, your eating habits, or your relationship with food, talk to a GP or a registered dietitian.
