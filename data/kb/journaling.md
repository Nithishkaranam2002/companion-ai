Journaling can help people manage stress, cope with difficult feelings, and notice patterns in their thoughts and moods. Tips for getting started:

- Try to write every day, even for just a few minutes.
- Keep it easy: have a pen and paper or a notes app ready.
- Write or draw whatever feels right; there is no correct structure.
- Use the journal as you see fit — it's private, and you decide whether to share it.

Tracking mood over time can help people notice what helps and what makes things harder. Journaling supports, but does not replace, care from a professional.
