---
title: Sleep Hygiene Basics
source: CDC — Sleep and Sleep Disorders, "Tips for Better Sleep"
url: https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/index.html
---
Most adults need 7 or more hours of sleep per night. Consistent habits help:

- Keep a regular schedule: go to bed and wake up at the same time each day, including weekends.
- Make the bedroom quiet, dark, relaxing, and at a comfortable temperature.
- Remove electronic devices such as TVs, computers, and phones from the bedroom, or stop using them 30–60 minutes before bed.
- Avoid large meals, caffeine, and alcohol before bedtime.
- Get some physical activity during the day; being active can help you fall asleep more easily at night.
- If you can't fall asleep after about 20 minutes, get up and do a quiet, relaxing activity in low light until you feel sleepy.

If sleep problems persist for weeks or affect daily life, talking with a healthcare provider is recommended.
