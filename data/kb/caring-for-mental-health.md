Self-care means doing things that help you live well and support your physical and mental health. The U.S. National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) says it can help manage stress, lower the risk of illness, and increase energy. Small acts every day can add up.

Self-care ideas from NIMH:

- **Move regularly.** A daily walk can help lift your mood, and small amounts of activity still count.
- **Eat regular, balanced meals and drink enough water.** Notice how caffeine and alcohol affect your mood and consider cutting back.
- **Make sleep a priority.** Keep a regular schedule and reduce screen time before bed.
- **Try a relaxing activity.** Breathing exercises, muscle relaxation, or meditation can help, as can scheduling enjoyable things like music, reading, or time outside.
- **Set goals and priorities.** Decide what must be done now and what can wait, say no to new tasks when you feel overloaded, and notice what you did get done each day.
- **Practice gratitude.** Remind yourself daily of specific things you're thankful for.
- **Focus on positivity.** Identify and challenge negative or unhelpful thoughts.
- **Stay connected.** Reach out to friends or family for emotional and practical support.

What works differs from person to person, so it may take some trial and error.

If you have severe or distressing symptoms lasting two weeks or more, such as trouble sleeping, appetite changes, or losing interest in things you usually enjoy, reach out to a health care provider.
