The NHS describes five evidence-based steps that can help you feel more positive and get more out of life:

1. **Connect with other people.** Good relationships build a sense of belonging and self-worth. Try a regular family meal, a day out with a friend you haven't seen in a while, lunch with a colleague, or a video call with someone far away. Try not to rely only on texts and messages.
2. **Be physically active.** Activity can lift mood and self-esteem and help you set and reach goals. You don't need hours in a gym; pick something you enjoy and fit it into your routine.
3. **Learn new skills.** Learning can boost confidence and give a sense of purpose. Cook a new dish, take on a new responsibility at work, try a DIY project, sign up for a course, or start a hobby like painting or a new sport.
4. **Give to others.** Small acts of kindness create feelings of reward and connection. Thank someone, ask how a friend is and really listen, help with a task, or volunteer locally.
5. **Pay attention to the present moment.** Noticing your thoughts, feelings, body and surroundings (mindfulness) can help you enjoy life more and understand yourself better.

If you are struggling to cope despite trying these steps, it is a good idea to talk to a GP or another health professional.
