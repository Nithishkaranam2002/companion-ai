---
title: Social Connection and Wellbeing
source: U.S. Surgeon General's Advisory, "Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation" (2023)
url: https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf
---
Social connection — the structure, function, and quality of our relationships — is a significant predictor of wellbeing. Loneliness and isolation are linked to higher risk of depression, anxiety, and other health problems.

Small, practical steps can strengthen connection:

- Reach out to a friend or family member for a few minutes each day.
- Minimize distractions, like phones, during conversations so you can be present.
- Join a group built around an interest — volunteering, a class, a club, or a faith community.
- Offer help or support to others; acts of service can build a sense of belonging.

If loneliness feels overwhelming or persistent, a counselor or mental health professional can help.
